## 2026-07-13 — ProdOps Framework Refactoring (Prompts 2–8)

### Summary

Complete refactoring of the ProdOps Framework to adopt the two-flow backlog model (Global: Portfolio; Local: Product). Introduced canonical backlog vocabulary, OBC lifecycle with 5 states, formal separation between modes (Upstream/Downstream) and journeys, and artifact governance.

### Scope

Exclusively documentary change — no application code was modified.

### Changes

**New backlog model (two flows):**
- Global: Global Tracking List → Business Intent Backlog → Roadmap → Platform Release
- Local: Repository Tracking List → Product Intent Backlog (via Premortem + Reliability Plan + Owner Approval)
- Convergence: Product Intent Backlog → Icebox → Iteration Backlog → Iteration Plan → Delivery

**New canonical terms added to glossary (glossary.md and .en.md):**
- Global Tracking List, Business Intent Backlog, Repository Tracking List, Product Intent Backlog, Icebox, Iteration Backlog, Iteration Plan

**OBC — lifecycle with 5 states:**
- Draft → Committed → Implemented → Operational → Archived
- OBC born at the Business Intent Backlog (global flow) or Product Intent Backlog (local flow)

**Modes × Journeys:**
- Upstream = exploration mode (no mandatory gates, vibecoding OK, OBC as memory)
- Downstream = commitment mode (mandatory Skills, OBC as operational contract)
- Journeys (Discovery, Delivery, Operation, Assessment, Diligence) exist independently of modes
- 4 Upstream closure outcomes: Discard, Archive, Return to BIB, Promote to Downstream
