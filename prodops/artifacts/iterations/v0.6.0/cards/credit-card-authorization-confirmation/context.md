## Contract

Endpoint(s) e essenciais de request/response:

- `POST /invoices` — body: `{ billingType: "CREDIT_CARD", orderId, amount, ... }` (hosted: no card data) → 201 `{ invoiceId, status: "OPEN", hostedPaymentUrl }`
- `POST /invoices` — body: `{ billingType: "CREDIT_CARD", creditCardToken, remoteIp, ... }` (tokenized) → 201 `{ invoiceId, status: "OPEN"|"AUTHORIZED"|"AWAITING_RISK_ANALYSIS" }`
