# Business

Artifacts that define what the product commits to deliver — business intents, observable contracts, and behavior specifications.

| Subdirectory | Content |
|---|---|
| [intents/](intents/) | Business Intents — business intentions explored in Upstream before becoming OBCs |
| [obcs/](obcs/) | Observable Business Contracts — committed observable contracts, source of truth for expected behavior |
| [bdd/](bdd/) | BDD Features — committed Gherkin specifications that govern the TDD cycle in Downstream |
