### What changed?

Two corrections coming out of the lead developer's review on the Finish
refinement PR.

**1. The `sast` gate became `code-analysis`.** The name promised less than — and
something different from — what the gate delivers: the provisioned quality gate
evaluates violations, duplication **and** security hotspots — maintainability and
reliability, not security alone. SonarSource itself describes the product as a
*static analysis tool*; SAST is a subset of what it does. Three identifiers
renamed together:

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| `gates.sast` (manifest) | `gates.code-analysis` |
| `scripts/check-sast.sh` | `scripts/check-code-analysis.sh` |
| quality gate `prodops-sast` | `prodops-code-analysis` |

**2. v0.15.0 was opened.** The Finish refinement had been running without an
iteration — `iteration-plan.md` read "no active iteration". The work was
decomposed into DS-62, DS-63, and DS-64 under issue #11 and recorded as the
active iteration.

### Why?

**On the rename.** Calling the gate SAST implied that a red result is always a
security finding — when it may be a code smell or duplication. The ambiguity had
a practical cost: the repository uses "SCA" for `gates.dependencies` (Snyk) in
the literal sense of *Software Composition Analysis*, and adopting the acronym
for Sonar as well would make the same term mean two different things in
neighboring gates of the same manifest.

**On the iteration.** ProdOps infrastructure work does belong to an iteration in
this repository's pattern — v0.8.0, v0.11.0, and v0.12.0 are precedents, none of
them touching `api/src`. v0.8.0 states it in its objective: *"this iteration does
not deliver product — it delivers reliability and traceability of the delivery
framework itself"*. There was no reason for the Finish refinement to sit outside
the model.

### Boundary of the rename

"SAST" was **kept** where it describes CodeQL, which is SAST in the strict sense,
and in the explanation of why the gate no longer carries that name. The three
tools are now explicitly distinguished in the skills:

- `code-analysis` (Sonar, local) — analyzes the source code
- `dependencies` (Snyk, SCA) — analyzes third-party libraries
- CodeQL (SAST, remote in CI) — analyzes the source code for vulnerabilities
